# Measurement contract

Use the deepest trustworthy outcome, not the easiest available count.

## Canonical stages

1. Impressions
2. Three-second views when video is used
3. Link clicks
4. Landing-page views
5. Leads persisted in the CRM
6. Qualified leads or confirmed bookings
7. Attended appointments or completed sales calls
8. Purchases or won deals

Each stage must name its source system and time window.

## Rules

- Missing UTM or click ID means unknown attribution, not organic.
- A Meta action count may lag. Reconcile it with the CRM.
- Use equal time boundaries and the ad account timezone.
- Use lifetime or cohort-complete economics when conversion takes days or weeks.
- State small-sample limits. Do not manufacture certainty.
- A higher cost per lead can be healthy when lead quality improves.
- Do not scale beyond the team's ability to respond to leads.

## Minimum report

- Spend, currency and window
- Count at every available stage
- Conversion rate between adjacent stages
- Cost per lead and cost per verified outcome
- Revenue and gross return when verified
- Largest measured leak
- Unknown or untrusted fields
- One recommended next action
